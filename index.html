Road to the final

How the tournament works

  1. Super Series

    2 to 6 October, Windhoek

    The 2nd, 3rd and 4th-placed teams from the World Cup Qualifier play each other once. The winner takes the last group stage place.

  2. Group stage

    7 to 24 October

    Two groups of six. The top three in each group go through, plus the better of the two fourth-placed teams.

  3. Super 7

    25 October to 14 November

    Seven teams start again on zero points and play each other once. The top four reach the semi-finals.

  4. Semis and final

    17, 18 and 21 November

    1st plays 4th and 2nd plays 3rd. Both semis and the final have a reserve day.

Super 7 slots are pre-seeded so fans can plan travel early. If a seeded team fails to qualify, the team that knocks it out takes its slot and its fixtures.
